Privacy Policy — Futbol Factory Money Dashboard

The Futbol Factory Money Dashboard is an internal tool used only by Futbol Factory LLC to view its own financial records. It is not offered to other businesses or the public.

What it accesses. With the owner's permission, the dashboard reads Futbol Factory's own QuickBooks Online accounting data (accounts, transactions and reports) and its own Stripe payment records.

How it's stored. Data is stored in a private, access-controlled database. QuickBooks connection tokens are stored server-side and are never shown in a browser. Only signed-in, approved Futbol Factory owners can view the dashboard.

Sharing. The data is not sold, shared or disclosed to any third party.

Disconnecting. The connection can be removed at any time from QuickBooks (Settings → Apps). Stored data is deleted on request.
